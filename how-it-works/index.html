How it works

From public record to an evidence-aware review package.

The planned Florida construction experience is a six-stage review workflow, not an autonomous outreach system. A person remains responsible for relevance and every next step.

Six-stage review workflow

What GrowthAgent AI is being built to do.

Turn candidate public construction records into organized, evidence-aware opportunities for a person to review. Every stage remains a planned product direction under validation.

01

Discover

Evaluate candidate public construction records from source categories approved for validation.

Permit, planning, procurement, and other approved public-source categories

02

Preserve evidence

Keep the originating record, jurisdiction, dates, documents, and evidence slot beside the opportunity.

Designed to retain source context before any fit decision

03

Classify

Map each fictional example to the most relevant construction division, trade, and representative scope.

Division classification helps reduce irrelevant review

04

Match fit

Compare division, trade, service area, project type, keywords, exclusions, recency, and evidence.

Customer-defined priorities remain planned configuration

05

Explain rank

Show why an opportunity appears through understandable fit and exclusion signals.

Explainable review signals instead of an unexplained score

06

Human review

A person confirms relevance, reviews the evidence, and controls any future next step.

No fabricated contacts or automatic outreach are represented

Controlled product phases

What happens after human review.

The current website stops at internal review. Any delivery, enrichment, outreach, reply, or meeting workflow is a future capability requiring separate owner approval and evidence.

Current website scope

Opportunity review foundation

  • Public-source discovery
  • Evidence preservation
  • Division classification
  • Fit explanation
  • Internal human review

Private-pilot status is not claimed because this repository contains no owner-approved evidence for that label.

Future owner-approved capabilities

Controlled next-step workflows

  • Customer-specific delivery
  • Contact-path enrichment using evidence
  • Outreach drafting
  • Approval workflows
  • Reply tracking
  • Follow-up and meeting workflows

Future means not active. Each capability requires owner approval, evidence, privacy review, and operating controls.
